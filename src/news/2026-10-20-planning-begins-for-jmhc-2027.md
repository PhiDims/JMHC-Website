The Central Working Committee has begun planning the 2027 edition of the Jos Mental Health Conference.

The theme, dates, venue, speakers and programme will be published on this website as soon as the committee approves them. Organisations interested in sponsoring or partnering can already [send an enquiry](/partner-with-us/#partner-form).
