---
title: JMHC website goes live
date: 2026-10-25
summary: The conference now has a permanent home online for announcements, registration, partnership and the record of every edition.
---
The Jos Mental Health Conference now has a permanent home online at josmhconference.com.

The website will carry announcements about the 2027 edition, registration, the call for abstracts, and ways to sponsor, partner or give. After each edition it will also keep the programme, speakers, photographs, media coverage and the conference report, so the work of every edition stays on record.

Join the mailing list at the bottom of any page to hear first when dates, venue and theme are announced.
